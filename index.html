Question 01 / 10
What is the main role of a Payment Gateway in e-commerce?
The payment gateway is the "digital middleman" — it connects all parties and verifies every transaction before money moves.
Question 02 / 10
Which payment method lets customers buy NOW and pay in installments LATER?
BNPL like SPayLater and Atome allow installment payments — but carry serious debt and overspending risks!
Question 03 / 10
What does SSL Encryption protect?
SSL encrypts data in transit. Look for https:// — the "s" means your data is safe from hackers.
Question 04 / 10
Aiman receives a fake SMS asking for his OTP. This is an example of...?
Phishing uses urgency and fake messages to steal your OTP. No real bank will EVER ask for your OTP!
Question 05 / 10
What is the correct order of an e-commerce payment flow?
Customer → Platform → Payment Gateway → Bank/E-Wallet → Seller receives payment.
Question 06 / 10
Tokenization replaces your card number with...?
Your real card (4532 7891...) becomes useless token (A7X9-B21Z). Hackers can't use it even if stolen!
Question 07 / 10
Which body regulates digital payment systems in Malaysia?
Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) is Malaysia's central bank and primary regulator of all digital payment systems.
Question 08 / 10
Key difference — Payment Gateway vs Payment Processor?
Gateway (iPay88) authorizes at the front-end. Processor (Visa Network) moves the actual money at the back-end.
Question 09 / 10
Which payment type is most linked to "forgotten subscription" risk?
Subscriptions auto-renew monthly or yearly. Students forget and keep getting charged!
Question 10 / 10
BNPL systems most directly encourage which consumer behaviour?
BNPL removes the "pain" of spending — impulse buying and debt accumulation risk.
